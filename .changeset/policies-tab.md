Agent wallet rules become Policies on screen, with a total and an active period. The wire names stay `rules`.

Client: an `AomiRule` amount is a total that never resets. A rule gains `active_from` (set by the server) and `active_until` (unix seconds; left out for always on; a write sends it). An ended rule stays in the list. `RULES_ERROR_CODES.periodInvalid` (`rules_period_invalid`) refuses an end that is not in the future. Breaking: `AomiWalletRules.usage`, `AomiWalletRules.window_ends_at` and the type `AomiRuleUsage` are removed; nothing counts spend any more.

Client, per-rule on-chain enforcement (one on-chain role or grant per rule, not per wallet and network). Breaking:

- `AomiWalletRules.enforcement` has one `AomiRulesEnforcement` entry per rule and provider: `{ rule, provider, name, network, state, ready, left, expires_at, account, deployment_tx, note, signer }`. `rule` is the rule's key; `left` is what the chain still lets through (a decimal string in the rule's base units, or null); `expires_at` is the chain's end for the rule in unix seconds (an estimate); `account` holds the funds; `deployment_tx` is what the owner's wallet returned (null for a signed grant). `rules`, `holds` and the per-asset `left` map are removed.
- `AomiRulesEnforceRequest` needs `rule`, the entry's rule key, so `prepareEnforcement` and `confirmEnforcement` act on one rule.
- `AomiRulesAction` is a union: besides `transaction` there is `typed_data` (`network`, `typed_data: AomiRulesTypedData`), EIP-712 data the owner's EVM wallet signs, sending nothing. Numbers above 2^53 in its `message` are decimal strings; convert the fields its types declare as integers before signing.
- `confirmEnforcement` takes `AomiRulesConfirmRequest`: the same body plus an optional `signature` (0x hex), needed for `typed_data` and not for `transaction`.
- The client refuses an action kind it does not know; a caller must also refuse an action whose `network` is not the entry's.
- New `RULES_ERROR_CODES`: `notEnforceable`, `ruleNotFound`, `bindingNotFound`, `providerNotFound`, `deploymentTooLarge`, `stepSignatureRequired`, `stepSignatureInvalid`, `stepSignatureUnexpected`.
- Needs a backend with per-rule enforcement; this client refuses to parse an older one's entries.

Widget: Settings gains a Policies tab (`policies`, after Connections) listing every agent wallet's policies: the two switches; the picker (`token / amount / chain / to`, several chains at once, an optional active period); each policy's on-chain enforcement (provider, state, what the chain still lets through, "Sign to deploy", a link to the deployment or the owner's account); signing a Swig transaction or an EVM typed-data grant with the owner's wallet (refusing a step for another network than the policy's); waiting policy changes at the top; and the policy change authority. The agent wallet's Manage sheet replaces its Rules section with a count of connections and policies linking to those tabs. A rule change reads "Policy change" there and on `/rules/<change id>`.

CLI: `aomi rules` no longer prints a `used` line.

Client and widget, the picker's hint: `AomiWalletRules.providers` (type `AomiRulesProvider`, `{ provider, name, network, holds, note }`) lists each on-chain provider and network the backend has switched on that could hold the wallet's rules, with or without a rule on it. `holds` is `total_and_period` or `total_period_and_destination`. A backend that does not send it reads as an empty list. The picker names the provider for each chosen chain ("None" only where no provider covers the network) and says when a provider leaves the destination to Aomi.

Client and widget, the owner sends their own signed revocation. Where Aomi cannot send a stored revocation itself (no account-abstraction route on the chain, which is every mainnet today), `AomiRulesAction` gains `evm_transaction` (`network: "eip155:<id>"`, `to`, `data`, `value`, all `0x` hex): a plain transaction the owner's EVM wallet sends, redeeming the stored revocation at the DelegationManager. The client reads it strictly (20-byte `to`, hex `data` and `value`, an `eip155` network) and still refuses unknown kinds. `confirmEnforcement` for it takes no signature and answers the rules unchanged until the backend's node shows the grant disabled. A removed policy in that state reads "Your revocation is signed. Send it from your wallet to take the grant off chain, or wait for Aomi to send it where the network supports that." with a "Send revocation" button. The widget switches the wallet to the policy's chain and sends the transaction from the wallet that owns the policy through the wallet kit's EVM send (no sponsor, no account abstraction). It refuses another network than the entry's, a `to` that is not a plain 0x address, any value, another account, and a wallet of another family. Needs a backend with the send step; an older one never answers this kind.

Widget, the owner's vault withdraw. A wallet with a MetaMask vault (an `enforcement` entry of provider `metamask`, live or removed, on Ethereum, OP, Base or Base Sepolia) gets one Vault row in its Policies card per vault and chain: the vault's short address, its balances read from the chain (the catalog's tokens on that chain and ETH, zero balances left out) and a Withdraw button. Withdraw opens inline: pick a token, enter an amount or press Max, then Sign and send. The owner's wallet switches to the vault's chain, signs a one-redemption delegation to itself valid for 10 minutes, and sends the transaction that redeems it, paying the network fee. A vault with no code yet says "Nothing to withdraw yet. Your vault is created with your first trade." The signature is never stored or sent to Aomi. With another wallet connected, Withdraw is disabled with "Connect … to sign."
