`CommitHandle.members` lists every batch member as its own handle, and `CommitHandle.groupView` is the view as its card shows it. The sign page for a multi-transaction request now gives each card its own member's progress, keeps the list and review up until the batch settles, sends every leg from one Send, and drops "View on explorer". The account menu's open wallet group shows about three wallets and scrolls, with one "Add a wallet" row.
