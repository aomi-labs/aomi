Agent wallets in the wallet menu and Settings: create (portal only), rename, pause, archive, deposit (QR) and send. One wallet view (search, kind chips, three rows a page) lists every wallet with each agent wallet's lane (Ask on Aomi, Ask in the client, Instant) on consent, the terminal and Telegram link pages, Settings › Connections and each wallet's "Who can use it"; sign-in wallets show their fixed "Sign in <wallet>". Consent pre-fills a client's lanes and writes nothing when they are unchanged. Sign-in wallets get a Manage sheet (Receive, Rename, who can use it); an address row under a wallet only copies, and the wallet's ⋯ opens with Manage. The sign page puts "Connect <wallet>" in the Submit slot until the signer is connected. Approve in the sidebar and on the sign page (`CommitSignPanel`), which, like send, follows the whole batch. `TelegramLinkPanel` links a Telegram account. Thread rows mark chats started elsewhere (scheduled, MCP/API, CLI/SDK, Telegram, inside an app). Removed: provider-delegated and client-side automatic signing (`usePrivyDelegation`, `PrivyDelegationProvider`).
